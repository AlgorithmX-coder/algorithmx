Because you are a consumer buying at a distance, the **Consumer Contracts (Information,

operate it** (this is a decision for you, explained below):

> **[DECISION REQUIRED — digital-content cancellation model]**
> **Option A — Instant access, with waiver.** At checkout you can ask the customer to
> (1) **expressly consent** to the course being made available immediately, and (2)
> **acknowledge that by doing so they lose their 14-day right to cancel** once access
> has begun. If you capture both (and send confirmation), you may give instant lifetime

> unfair, *especially* on a children's product.
> **Option B — Honour the full 14 days.** You wait 14 days (or until the customer
> expressly waives) before treating the sale as final, and refund cancellations within
> 14 days. More consumer-friendly and simpler to defend, but means a refund window on
> every sale.
