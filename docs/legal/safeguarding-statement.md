# Safeguarding Statement — AlgorithmX Ltd

**Last updated:** [INSERT: date] · **Version:** Draft 0.1

## 1. Our commitment

AlgorithmX Ltd is committed to the safety and wellbeing of every child who uses our
platform. Cyber Heroes Academy is built for children, and we treat their safety,
privacy, and best interests as the first consideration in how we design, run, and improve
the service. This commitment reflects the principles of the ICO's **Children's Code** and
good safeguarding practice for online services aimed at children.

## 2. What kind of service we are

AlgorithmX provides a **self-service, online educational platform**. We are **not** a
school, nursery, or regulated childcare provider, and we do **not** have direct
supervisory care of children in person. A **parent or guardian** sets up and controls the
account and is responsible for supervising their child's use of the platform. This
statement describes the safeguarding measures appropriate to an online product of this
kind.

## 6. How we handle a safeguarding disclosure

If we receive information suggesting a child may be at risk of harm, we will:
1. **Take it seriously and act promptly** — we do not ignore or dismiss concerns.
2. **Record** the concern factually and securely, sharing it only with those who need to
   know.
3. **Assess** whether the child may be at risk and what action is appropriate.
4. **Refer to the proper authorities** where appropriate — for example the police, or
   local authority children's social care — and cooperate with them. We may make a
   referral **without delay** where a child appears to be at immediate risk, and may do
   so without parental consent where seeking it could increase risk to the child.
5. **Protect data appropriately** — safeguarding information is sensitive; we handle it in
   line with data-protection law, which permits sharing personal data where necessary to
   protect a child.
