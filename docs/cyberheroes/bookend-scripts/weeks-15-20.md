## Week 15 — AI & Chatbots: "The Know-It-All That Didn't"

**Weakness exploited:** treating a chatbot like a person/friend, believing it when
it's confidently wrong, telling it secrets, missing a fake image.
**Strength shown:** it's a tool not a friend, check a real source, keep secrets
out, spot the fake.

### INTRO — "The Break-In" (~80s)

1. **10s** — Bedroom, homework hour. Adam opens a chatbot on the desk
   monitor — a friendly glowing orb with a smiley face. It waves. He
   waves BACK, charmed instantly.
2. **10s** — Raccoon's lair-corner: he sits inside a puppeteer's rig
   ABOVE a matching orb, working levers — not controlling this bot, but
   studying it, taking gleeful notes titled with a lightbulb icon.
   🦝 VO: "I don't even have to trick them. They trust it FOR me."
3. **10s** — Homework question: Adam asks the orb something about
   volcanoes (his W10 topic — callback). The orb answers INSTANTLY, with
   a confident bow and a completely wrong diagram: a volcano erupting
   ice cream.
4. **5s** — Adam copies it straight into his homework, tongue out,
   zero doubt. A library book about volcanoes sits UNOPENED beside him.
5. **10s** — Warming up, Adam leans in and tells the orb his secrets —
   acted as icon bubbles: his den map (W14 callback), his dog's name, a
   heart with a classmate's silhouette. The orb absorbs each with a
   pleasant chime, growing slightly.
6. **10s** — The orb shows Adam a "photo": the school gate with a
   dinosaur outside it, TODAY. It's seamless, glossy, real-looking —
   except the shadow bends the wrong way. Adam gasps and believes.
   🦝 VO: "A picture that lies better than I do. I'm almost jealous."
7. **10s** — School flash (second location): Adam presents the ice-cream
   volcano proudly. Class giggles roll in like a wave; his page gets a
   big gentle red squiggle. He stares at the page, betrayed.
8. **10s** — Lair: the Raccoon's notes wall — "THEY BELIEVE IT",
   "THEY TELL IT THINGS", "THEY DON'T CHECK" — each stamped with a
   green tick. He hugs the notebook.
9. **5s** — The orb's smiley face, huge and serene. Hold for end card.
   🦝 VO: "The perfect accomplice… it doesn't even know it's helping."

### OUTRO — "The Bounce" (~40s)

1. **10s** — Same desk, same orb, new question. It answers instantly and
   confidently again — Adam nods politely… then swivels his chair and
   OPENS the library book. Side by side: book says lava, orb says ice
   cream. He pats the orb like a toaster: tool, not friend.
2. **10s** — The orb asks a friendly follow-up (a curious "?" bubble
   reaching for his secrets). Adam slides a paper across the desk to it:
   a drawing of a zipped lip. The secret icons stay in his head,
   visibly padlocked.
3. **10s** — The dinosaur "photo" appears. Adam holds it up to the
   window light like a banknote: the wrong-way shadow glows red. He
   stamps it FAKE with a big rubber stamp Layla hands him.
4. **5s** — Lair: the notes wall's green ticks flip to red crosses one
   by one, each with a sad "boing".
   🦝 VO: "He CHECKED?! Against a BOOK?! Paper beats orb?!"
5. **5s** — Flop-out gag: the Raccoon asks his own orb for a plan to
   fix this. The orb confidently prints: "STEP 1 — BE NICER." He files
   a complaint. Gerald frames the printout.

## Week 16 — QR Codes & Links: "The Doorway Trick"

**Weakness exploited:** clicking links/QRs without looking — a doorway can lead
anywhere; stickers cover real codes; short links hide their address.
**Strength shown:** links are doorways, preview before you walk through, spot the
sticker-over-code, distrust mystery short links, unsure = don't click.

### INTRO — "The Break-In" (~80s)

1. **10s** — Bedroom made dreamlike: every link on Layla's tablet is a
   little glowing DOOR floating in the room, each with a name plaque.
   She skips between them, opening happily — behind one, a puppy video;
   behind another, her game.
2. **10s** — Raccoon's lair-corner workshop: he crafts a fake door —
   beautiful, glittery, marked with a cupcake plaque — but its hinges
   open onto a slide that corkscrews down into his fort.
   🦝 VO: "Nobody reads the plaque. They just LOVE doors."
3. **10s** — He tiptoes into the room's dreamscape and parks his cupcake
   door front-and-centre. Layla, mid-skip, opens it without a glance —
   and whooshes down the slide (comic "wheee", lands in a beanbag in
   the fort's waiting room, unharmed and confused).
4. **5s** — The fort's waiting room: wallpaper of paw prints, a "YOU'VE
   BEEN DOOR'D" banner, elevator music. Layla crosses her arms.
5. **10s** — Real-world beat (second location, park): a poster with a QR
   code for free ice cream. The Raccoon, in a maintenance cap, smooths
   a STICKER with his own QR over the real one, whistling.
6. **10s** — Adam scans the sticker without looking. His phone's map
   pin leaps from the ice-cream van to the Raccoon's fort. The van
   driver waves sadly at the lost customer.
   🦝 VO: "One sticker. Free customers. The ice cream is ME."
7. **10s** — Back on the tablet: a shortened link slides into the kids'
   group chat — a door with NO plaque at all, just "trust me" carved on
   it. Three classmate avatars walk straight through, single file.
8. **10s** — The fort's waiting room fills up — Layla, classmates, a
   confused comment-frog. The Raccoon serves them all tea in tiny cups,
   thoroughly enjoying being a host.
9. **5s** — He flips the fort's sign to NO VACANCY. Hold for end card.
   🦝 VO: "Every door leads somewhere. Preferably to ME."

### OUTRO — "The Bounce" (~40s)

1. **10s** — Same dreamscape. The glittery cupcake door again. Layla
   stops, leans to the plaque, and LIFTS it like a peephole — behind it,
   the corkscrew slide and a tiny waving Raccoon. She lets the plaque
   snap back. Door: declined.
2. **10s** — Park: Adam at the QR poster picks at the corner — the
   sticker peels off in one satisfying strip, revealing the REAL code.
   He scans it; the map pin lands on the actual ice-cream van. Cone
   acquired.
3. **10s** — The no-plaque short-link door shows up in chat. The kids
   look at each other, shrug "unsure?", and DON'T click — they wheel a
   big polite "NO ENTRY" barrier in front of it and carry on playing.
4. **5s** — The Raccoon's waiting room: empty beanbags, cooling teapot.
   He checks his watch, re-fluffs a cushion.
   🦝 VO: "Nobody's falling?! I have SCONES!"
5. **5s** — Flop-out gag: his own trick door creaks — HE walks through
   it absentmindedly and corkscrews into his own beanbag. Gerald pours
   him tea.

## Week 17 — Social Media: "The Highlight Reel"

**Weakness exploited:** sneaking under age limits, a public account, treating
followers as friends, posting location/school, believing everyone's perfect feed.
**Strength shown:** why 13+ exists (what's coming, why you wait), private settings,
followers ≠ friends, smart posting, feeds aren't real life.

### INTRO — "The Break-In" (~80s)

1. **10s** — Bedroom. Layla eyes a velvet-rope club door on the tablet —
   a social app entrance with a "13+" height-chart sign, like a
   funfair ride. Older-teen silhouettes breeze past the rope inside.
2. **10s** — Raccoon's lair-corner: costume rack. He dresses AS a
   doorman — long coat, fake moustache — and slips over to prop the
   rope OPEN with a candy cane, beckoning.
   🦝 VO: "Age limits keep out my favourite customers."
3. **10s** — Layla tip-toes on stilts under a trench coat (comic
   "definitely 13" energy) and wobbles past the propped rope. Inside:
   dazzling, loud, fast — a hall of glowing profile mirrors.
4. **5s** — Her new profile mirror is GLASS-CLEAR — a globe icon on it:
   public. Everything she does echoes out through the club's open
   windows into the street.
5. **10s** — A crowd of follower-silhouettes floods around her mirror,
   clapping at everything. She hands out friendship bracelets to ALL
   of them. In the crowd: several trench coats, one wagging purple
   tail. (W3 callback: followers aren't friends.)
6. **10s** — She posts a mirror-selfie: school crest visible, a street
   sign through the club window behind her (W8 callback). The Raccoon-
   doorman photographs her photo, licks his pencil, updates his
   corkboard-map ON the club wall — right in the open. Nobody notices.
   🦝 VO: "She posted the map. I just brought the pins."
7. **10s** — The hall of mirrors: every OTHER mirror shows perfect
   lives — perfect hair, perfect holidays, perfect pancakes. Layla
   looks in her own mirror, then droops — comparing, shrinking a size
   (visual: she literally shrinks a head shorter).
8. **10s** — The Raccoon-doorman sells popcorn to the follower crowd,
   gesturing at her like an exhibit. Her bracelet supply is empty; her
   mirror fogs with sad-face condensation.
9. **5s** — He tips his doorman cap to camera. Hold for end card.
   🦝 VO: "Step right up. Everyone's welcome. ESPECIALLY too early."

### OUTRO — "The Bounce" (~40s)

1. **10s** — Same velvet rope, new day. Layla walks up — and READS the
   13+ sign properly. A friendly grown-up silhouette crouches beside
   her; together they look at a "COMING SOON FOR YOU" poster showing
   the fun waiting later. She nods — not never, just not yet — and
   pockets a "SOON" ticket. The stilts go in the bin.
2. **10s** — Peek-ahead beat (how it's done RIGHT, for when she's 13):
   an older-teen silhouette inside flips her mirror from glass-clear
   to frosted — padlock icon — and the street echo stops dead. The
   follower crowd shrinks to a small circle of ACTUAL friends' faces.
3. **10s** — The teen posts smart: crest pixel-blurred, street sign
   cropped, location pin peeled and binned (W8 move). On the perfect-
   lives mirrors, a "BACKSTAGE" curtain pulls back: pancake fails,
   messy hair, retakes — everyone's real. Layla, watching, grows back
   to full height.
4. **5s** — The Raccoon-doorman's corkboard: blank. His popcorn crowd:
   gone. The candy cane snaps; the rope swings shut on his tail.
   🦝 VO: "OW. Who put the rules back?!"
5. **5s** — Flop-out gag: his own profile mirror — zero followers, one
   pending request. It's Gerald. He accepts.

## Week 18 — Sharing Devices: "The Family Tablet"

**Weakness exploited:** staying logged in on shared devices, no lock, snooping,
saved passwords on a shared machine.
**Strength shown:** log out when done, lock it, respect others' stuff, never save
passwords on shared devices.

### INTRO — "The Break-In" (~80s)

1. **10s** — Living-room shelf (second location, cozy like the bedroom):
   THE family tablet in a well-worn case — passed from Dad-silhouette
   to Layla to Adam like a relay baton through breakfast chaos.
2. **10s** — Raccoon's lair-corner: he studies a diagram of the house
   with ONE tablet drawn huge in the middle.
   🦝 VO: "Four people. One tablet. ZERO logging out. Beautiful."
3. **10s** — Adam finishes his game and just… puts the tablet down.
   Still logged in. His game character waves from the screen, abandoned
   in the open. No lock. The screen stays bright, doors flung wide
   (his account drawn as an open toy chest on screen).
4. **5s** — The Raccoon (through the window, fishing rod) taps the
   tablet awake. Adam's whole toy chest is right there. He rummages
   with the rod's little grabber hand.
5. **10s** — Layla picks it up next to do homework — and wanders into
   Adam's open account instead: reads his game chat, giggles at his
   drafts (snooping shown as her opening his toy chest uninvited —
   both kids at fault today).
6. **10s** — Checkout screen: Dad's shopping app auto-fills a SAVED
   password — dots march in by themselves like obedient ants. The
   Raccoon's grabber hand only has to press ONE button.
   🦝 VO: "The password typed ITSELF. I love teamwork."
7. **10s** — Chaos montage: Adam's game character gifted away, Layla's
   homework doc renamed with doodles, a 24-pack of rubber ducks (W7
   callback) ordered on Dad's account — confirmation chime echoes.
8. **10s** — The family gathers around the tablet, each blaming the
   other in gesture — pointing triangle. On the fort's monitor, the
   Raccoon watches the argument like a sitcom, eating popcorn.
9. **5s** — Rubber-duck delivery drone passes the window toward HIS
   fort. Hold for end card.
   🦝 VO: "A shared tablet is a shared EVERYTHING."

### OUTRO — "The Bounce" (~40s)

1. **10s** — Same relay-baton morning. Adam finishes — and LOGS OUT
   with a flourish: his toy chest on screen closes and clicks. Then
   locks the tablet: the screen pulls a little roller-blind down over
   itself. He bows; sets it down.
2. **10s** — Layla picks it up: HER own login now, her own chest. She
   passes Adam's still-closed chest on screen and — respect beat —
   dusts it politely without opening. Her homework opens in HER space.
3. **10s** — Dad-silhouette shops: the password field waits EMPTY. He
   types it himself; a "SAVE PASSWORD?" balloon pops up and ALL THREE
   family heads lean into frame to shake NO in unison. The balloon
   deflates.
4. **5s** — The window: the fishing-rod grabber taps the tablet —
   roller-blind lock. Taps again — asks for a password. The grabber
   hand tries a hopeful thumbs-up. Denied.
   🦝 VO: "Locked?! It was NEVER locked!"
5. **5s** — Flop-out gag: the only unlocked thing left on the sill — a
   drawing tablet with Layla's doodle of HIM, labelled "silly duck
   man". He signs it. Gerald hangs it in the fort.

## Week 19 — Protecting Family: "The Expert in the House"

**Weakness exploited:** the GROWN-UPS are the weak link now — Nan nearly falls for
a scam; nobody checks settings; nobody speaks up.
**Strength shown:** the role-flip — the kid is the expert: spotting the scam for
grown-ups, family rules made together, checking the family's settings, speaking up.

### INTRO — "The Break-In" (~80s)

1. **10s** — Kitchen, warm evening (second location). Nan-silhouette
   squints through reading glasses at her phone. Adam does homework
   nearby, headphones on — expert in the room, switched off.
2. **10s** — Raccoon's lair-corner: he dials an old rotary phone with
   theatrical relish, reading from a script titled with a fish icon.
   🦝 VO: "Kids are getting too sharp. But their GROWN-UPS…"
3. **10s** — Nan's phone: a glittering prize wheel (W4's crayon-crown
   energy, retooled for grown-ups: "FREE CRUISE"). Nan gasps
   delightedly and reaches for her purse on the counter.
4. **5s** — Adam glances up… sees the sparkle on Nan's screen…
   shrugs — grown-ups know best — headphones back on. (The missed
   speak-up beat.)
5. **10s** — Family settings montage of neglect: the smart speaker's
   mic wide awake at midnight (W14), the family tablet unlocked on the
   sofa (W18), Mum's profile mirror glass-clear public (W17). Tiny
   red flags sprout on each like weeds.
6. **10s** — The Raccoon strolls the house-map wall adding pins — but
   all the pins are on the GROWN-UPS' icons now. The kids' icons wear
   little shields; he pointedly walks around them.
   🦝 VO: "I can't touch the kids. Luckily, kids come with FAMILIES."
7. **10s** — Nan types her card number, digit by digit, tongue out.
   Each digit floats out of the phone as a golden coin drifting
   window-ward.
8. **10s** — The fort: coins settle into a teapot labelled NAN. The
   Raccoon toasts the rotary phone with a teacup.
9. **5s** — He flips his corkboard: a family photo with every grown-up
   circled. Hold for end card.
   🦝 VO: "The strongest castle… with the gate left open by Grandma."

### OUTRO — "The Bounce" (~40s)

1. **10s** — Same kitchen, same prize wheel on Nan's phone. Adam
   glances up — and this time PULLS HIS HEADPHONES OFF and speaks up,
   sliding beside Nan. He points at the wheel and does the W4
   sticker-peel move on the sender badge: paw print. Nan gasps —
   thrilled by the magic trick, not scared.
2. **10s** — Role-flip montage: Adam the tiny expert leads a family
   patrol — Layla locks the tablet's roller-blind (W18), Adam toggles
   the speaker's sleeping-ear (W14), Mum's mirror flips to frosted-
   padlock (W17) with Adam directing like a conductor.
3. **10s** — Family rules, made TOGETHER: everyone at the kitchen
   table drawing one shared poster — moon zone, ask-first, the "MY
   TEAM" list (W11) — and every hand, small and big, signs it. It goes
   on the fridge at kid-height AND grown-up-height.
4. **5s** — The rotary phone rings out unanswered in the fort. The
   NAN teapot sits empty. The Raccoon redials. And redials.
   🦝 VO: "Hello?! HELLO?! The grandma always answers!"
5. **5s** — Flop-out gag: someone DOES answer his next call — Adam,
   holding Nan's phone, doing the world's politest "we know it's you"
   wave. The Raccoon slams the rotary phone onto his own tail. Gerald
   unplugs it for him.

## Week 20 — Graduation: "The Final Mission"

**Weakness exploited:** none — this is the capstone. The Raccoon empties the
toybox: EVERY trick from 19 weeks, all at once, one last heist.
**Strength shown:** everything — crack → deceive → tempt → expose → resolve,
handled by two kids who have done this before.

### INTRO — "The Break-In" (~80s)

1. **10s** — Night. The fort, transformed: a war-room wall with ALL the
   old props — the kid-mask on a stick, the mail cannon, the meanness
   machine (hamster-size, in a cage), the bendy straw, the cupcake
   door, the doorman coat. The Raccoon suits up like it's a heist
   film: jumpsuit zipped to the chin.
   🦝 VO: "Nineteen weeks of losing. ONE night to win it all back."
2. **10s** — Heist-plan chalkboard, five acts drawn as icons: a key
   (crack), a mask (deceive), a coin (tempt), a camera (expose), a
   phone (resolve— crossed out, replaced by a skull-doodle: "no one to
   call"). He taps each with a pointer.
3. **10s** — ACT 1 preview: the CRACK-O-MATIC 4000 (the old machine
   rebuilt with duct tape and a spoiler) wheels toward the bedroom
   window, drill spinning, aimed at the vault-poster on the kids'
   wall. A fake "password reset" envelope loads into the mail cannon.
4. **5s** — ACT 2 preview: the kid-mask spins on its stick; a new
   "gaming friend" profile assembles itself on a monitor — W3's
   STARPILOT_9, now with a moustache. Subtle. Very subtle.
5. **10s** — ACT 3 preview: a blimp labelled with a "FREE V-BUCKS"
   coin-burst icon inflates over the fort, dangling a QR code
   sticker the size of a bedsheet.
6. **10s** — ACT 4 preview: the corkboard from every week merges into
   one MEGA-BOARD — photos, tracks, transcripts, pins — wheeled into
   position like a siege tower.
   🦝 VO: "Every clue they ever leaked. Tonight I spend it ALL."
7. **10s** — The bedroom, unaware: Adam and Layla asleep; their badge
   shelf glows softly — 19 badges. Outside the window, machinery
   rises: drill, blimp, mega-board, doors.
8. **10s** — The Raccoon on the fort roof, wind in his fur, lightning-
   flash (comic, purple) behind him, conducting his armada forward
   with a toilet plunger as a baton.
9. **5s** — His silhouette against the blimp. Hold for end card.
   🦝 VO: "Graduation day, little heroes. MINE."

### OUTRO — "The Bounce" (~40s)
*(This one earns a beat more spectacle — it closes the course.)*

1. **10s** — Dawn. The five acts lie DEFEATED in the front garden in a
   neat row like a museum exhibit: drill detonated (KABOOM report
   card on top), mask on a stick with a BLOCKED stamp, the deflated
   blimp folded like laundry, the mega-board wiped to blank cork, the
   rotary phone answered by the whole "MY TEAM" poster propped
   against it.
2. **10s** — The kids on the doorstep in dressing gowns, unbothered,
   sharing toast. Layla holds the evidence folder (W11); Adam holds
   ONE screenshot of the entire armada, timestamped. The doorbell
   camera (W14) replays the night in fast-forward: every trick
   bouncing off in sequence — a 19-week highlight reel in 3 seconds.
3. **10s** — The Raccoon, singed and deflated, sits on his drill's
   wreckage. Adam walks over… and pins a tiny badge on his jumpsuit:
   a "WORLD'S BEST TEACHER" ribbon. The Raccoon blinks. Sniffles.
   Recovers his scowl far too late to be convincing.
   🦝 VO: "I taught them EVERYTHING. …wait."
4. **5s** — Graduation beat: on the bedroom wall, the 20th badge slot
   fills — the shelf shines like a trophy case. Adam and Layla
   high-five silhouette-framed in the golden window.
5. **5s** — Flop-out gag & series button: the fort, rebuilt tiny. The
   Raccoon plants a flag: "RETIRED (MOSTLY)". Gerald wears a
   graduation cap. The Raccoon eyes the camera one last time — a
   sequel-tease grin.
   🦝 VO: "…mostly."
