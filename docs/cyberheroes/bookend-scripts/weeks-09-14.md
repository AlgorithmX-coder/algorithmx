# Bookend Scripts — Weeks 9–14

Format: shot number · seconds · action. `🦝 VO:` lines are the only spoken
audio and are recorded in Callum's voice. See 00-production-canon.md.

## Week 9 — Apps & Downloads: "The Copycat Shop"

**Weakness exploited:** installing a copycat app from outside the official store
and blindly accepting greedy permissions.
**Strength shown:** official store only, spotting the copycat, asking "why does it
need that?", installing with a grown-up.

### INTRO — "The Break-In" (~80s)

1. **10s** — Bedroom. Layla wants the hot new pet game everyone plays. On
   the tablet, a bright official app-store storefront gleams — but a
   banner ad points to a side alley: "GET IT FREE — NO WAITING!"
2. **10s** — Raccoon's lair-corner: a market stall of knock-off app boxes.
   He paints one extra whisker onto a copied app icon — identical except
   the cat has crossed eyes.
   🦝 VO: "One whisker off. NOBODY counts whiskers."
3. **10s** — Layla taps the alley version. A permissions scroll unrolls to
   the FLOOR: microphone, camera, photos, contacts — icon after icon. She
   yawns and hits the big green tick without reading.
4. **5s** — The app installs; its crossed-eyed cat icon winks to itself.
5. **10s** — The game plays fine at first — but behind the tablet
   (X-ray view), tiny purple gremlins carry off photo albums and a
   glowing contact book through a back door in the case.
6. **10s** — Raccoon's corner: deliveries arrive on a conveyor — her
   photos, her contact list, a live feed of the bedroom ceiling from the
   camera. He files them into drawers labelled with icons.
   🦝 VO: "She said yes to EVERYTHING. So polite!"
7. **10s** — On the tablet, the real store shows the REAL pet game with a
   verified rosette, waving sadly like the ignored option it was.
8. **10s** — The copycat app sprouts popups over every other app like
   weeds; the tablet slows, wheezing with a little fever thermometer.
9. **5s** — Raccoon stamps the crossed-eyed cat onto his stall's
   bestseller chart. Hold for end card.
   🦝 VO: "Copy, paste, collect. Business is BOOMING."

### OUTRO — "The Bounce" (~40s)

1. **10s** — Same want, new day. Layla walks PAST the alley banner and
   opens the official storefront. Two identical cat icons side by side —
   she leans in and counts whiskers. The crossed-eyed one sweats.
2. **10s** — She taps the verified rosette one; the permissions scroll
   unrolls just THREE icons long. She reads each, frowns at one —
   "microphone? for a PET game?" — and toggles it off.
3. **10s** — She waves the tablet toward the door; a grown-up silhouette
   joins for the final INSTALL tap together. The real pet purrs to life.
4. **5s** — The alley stall: the copycat app is ejected from the tablet,
   knocking the Raccoon's knock-off boxes down like dominoes.
   🦝 VO: "She COUNTED?! Who counts whiskers?!"
5. **5s** — Flop-out gag: his gremlins return empty-handed except one item
   — the "no thanks" button, gift-wrapped. Gerald finds it hilarious.

## Week 10 — YouTube & Videos: "The Rabbit Hole"

**Weakness exploited:** autoplay chaining videos, believing everything, staying in
videos not made for you, reading stranger comments, losing track of time.
**Strength shown:** noticing the pull, leaving what's not for you, doubting wild
claims, ignoring the comment swamp.

### INTRO — "The Break-In" (~80s)

1. **10s** — Bedroom, homework open on the desk. Adam plays ONE fun video
   on the tablet: a cheerful volcano experiment. Sunlight through the
   window; a wall clock reads after-school time.
2. **10s** — Raccoon's lair-corner: he operates a literal conveyor of
   "NEXT UP" cards, greasing the rollers so each card slides in faster.
   🦝 VO: "They only ever choose the FIRST one. I choose the rest."
3. **10s** — The video ends; the next one slides in by itself — silly,
   fine. Then the next. Each thumbnail gets a little louder, a little
   weirder. Adam's homework pencil rolls off the desk, unnoticed.
4. **5s** — A thumbnail flashes an outrageous claim: a "MOON IS CHEESE —
   PROOF!" style graphic. Adam's eyes go saucer-wide. He believes.
5. **10s** — The next video is clearly NOT for kids — shadowy thumbnail,
   too-loud sting. The room's cozy colours drain a shade; Adam shrinks
   into the chair but doesn't leave the video.
6. **10s** — He scrolls the comments — a swamp of stranger-avatars,
   arguing raccoons in trench coats among them. One comment-frog leaps
   out and croaks misinformation in his face.
   🦝 VO: "The comments! Where strangers do my job for FREE."
7. **10s** — Time-lapse: sunlight slides across the floor and dies; the
   wall clock spins; the string lights come on by themselves. Adam
   hasn't moved. Thumbnail glow on his face.
8. **10s** — Raccoon's corner: a wall of hourglasses, each labelled with
   a kid's avatar. He flips Adam's again with a croupier's flourish.
9. **5s** — Push on Adam's hypnotised face lit by the screen. Hold for
   end card.
   🦝 VO: "Just one more. And one more. And one more…"

### OUTRO — "The Bounce" (~40s)

1. **10s** — Same setup, same volcano video. It ends; the NEXT UP card
   slides in — and Adam catches it mid-slide like a frisbee. He looks at
   the wall clock, then at his homework. He NOTICED the pull.
2. **10s** — The cheese-moon claim flashes. Adam smirks, holds up a
   library book about the actual moon, and taps it twice. The thumbnail
   deflates with a whoopee-cushion wheeze.
3. **10s** — A not-for-kids video slides in; Adam doesn't negotiate — he
   backs out instantly with a clean over-the-shoulder exit gesture, and
   the screen returns to bright cozy colours. The comment swamp burbles;
   he simply… doesn't open it. The frog waits. Nobody comes.
4. **5s** — Raccoon's corner: his conveyor jams; NEXT UP cards pile into
   a paper mountain that avalanche-buries him.
   🦝 VO: "He looked at a CLOCK! Who taught him CLOCKS?!"
5. **5s** — Flop-out gag: Adam's hourglass on the wall of hourglasses has
   tipped over, done — a sticker across it: outside-playing icon. Gerald
   wears a tiny sweatband.

## Week 11 — Emergency Protocol: "Who You Gonna Tell?"

**Weakness exploited:** a scared kid with NO plan — blames himself, deletes the
evidence, tells no one.
**Strength shown:** never your fault, a NAMED trusted-adult list, stop & block,
screenshot don't delete, Childline 0800 1111 exists.
*(Tone note: the "wrong turn" beats are acted by the RACCOON demonstrating his
dream scenario with a crash-test DUMMY kid — keeps real kids out of distress
while making the wrong moves unmistakable.)*

### INTRO — "The Break-In" (~80s)

1. **10s** — Raccoon's lair-corner, staged like a lecture hall. He wheels
   in a whiteboard titled with a crying-face icon and a crash-test dummy
   child in a hoodie propped on a chair, tablet in its lap.
   🦝 VO: "Welcome to MY masterclass: what I HOPE kids do."
2. **10s** — He slaps the whiteboard: LESSON 1 — a mirror icon. He turns
   the dummy's head to face a mirror and paints a red "MY FAULT" arrow
   pointing at its reflection.
3. **10s** — LESSON 2 — a bin icon. He grabs the dummy's tablet, shows a
   nasty-message card on screen, and mimes the dummy deleting it. The
   card shreds; he applauds the dummy's limp hand for it.
   🦝 VO: "No evidence, no problem — for ME."
4. **5s** — LESSON 3 — a zipped-lips icon. He zips an actual zip across
   the dummy's hood. Perfect silence. He chef-kisses.
5. **10s** — Montage on the whiteboard: he crosses out, one by one, a
   row of helper figures — parent, teacher, big sister, phone-helpline —
   drawing little cages over each.
6. **10s** — Wide: the lair dims to a single spotlight on the dummy,
   alone with a glowing tablet. The Raccoon tiptoes around it in delight.
7. **10s** — He stamps a diploma: "GRADUATE — SUFFERED IN SILENCE" and
   tucks it under the dummy's arm. Confetti made of shredded evidence.
8. **10s** — Through a telescope, he peers at the real bedroom across the
   way: Adam looking worried at a buzzing tablet. The Raccoon rubs his
   paws — a live candidate.
   🦝 VO: "And there's my next star pupil!"
9. **5s** — His grin through the telescope lens, fish-eyed. Hold for end
   card.

### OUTRO — "The Bounce" (~40s)

1. **10s** — The real bedroom. The same buzzing tablet — a nasty message
   card lands. Adam's face falls for ONE second… then he stands up
   straight. Above his desk: a hand-drawn "MY TEAM" poster — Mum, Coach
   Priya, Nan, and a bright phone icon with 0800 1111.
2. **10s** — Beat-for-beat counter: he does NOT look in the mirror — he
   flips the red arrow around so it points out the window (not my
   fault). Then STOP & BLOCK: one calm tap; the sender's avatar is
   bricked over.
3. **10s** — Screenshot, don't delete: he presses the buttons; a camera-
   click freezes the nasty card in an evidence frame, which he slides
   into a folder marked with a shield.
4. **5s** — He walks straight to the door and hands tablet + evidence to
   Mum's silhouette; Layla arrives with juice. The room is FULL — the
   exact opposite of the spotlight-alone dummy.
   🦝 VO: "A LIST?! He had a LIST?!"
5. **5s** — Flop-out gag: the Raccoon's dummy, forgotten in the lair,
   slumps over onto HIS diploma. Even the dummy's unimpressed. Gerald
   has joined "MY TEAM" — there's a potato drawn on Adam's poster.

## Week 12 — Digital Footprint: "Tracks in the Snow"

**Weakness exploited:** thinking posts vanish — leaving a careless trail that
spreads and lasts.
**Strength shown:** tracks last, your future self is watching, make GOOD tracks,
check your trail.

### INTRO — "The Break-In" (~80s)

1. **10s** — Bedroom transformed by imagination: soft snow falls INSIDE
   the room (dream-logic), a white blanket over the floor. Layla in
   wellies stomps around posting from her phone — each post drops a
   bright pawprint-like TRACK in the snow behind her.
2. **10s** — Raccoon's lair-corner: he waxes a sled labelled with a
   magnifying-glass icon, puts on a woolly hat.
   🦝 VO: "Fresh tracks! And snow that NEVER melts."
3. **10s** — Layla posts carelessly: a grumpy jab at a friend (storm-cloud
   track), a silly embarrassing dance (banana-peel track), an overshare
   (open-diary track). She skips away; the tracks stay, glowing.
4. **5s** — She looks back once, waves a hand — expecting the tracks to
   fade like breath on glass. They don't. She shrugs; skips on.
5. **10s** — The Raccoon sleds along her trail, photographing each track
   and duplicating the photos — copies whirl up like snowflakes and
   settle on OTHER rooftops (spreading).
6. **10s** — Time-jump gag: a cardboard "5 YEARS LATER" sign on a stick.
   TALL-Layla silhouette (older, backpack) walks the same snowfield —
   and trips over her own banana-peel track, still frozen there.
   🦝 VO: "Old tracks trip the FUTURE you. Delicious."
7. **10s** — The Raccoon opens a museum in his fort: "GALLERY OF
   FOOTPRINTS", every careless track framed and lit. He sells tickets to
   the comment-frogs from Week 10.
8. **10s** — Wide aerial: Layla's trail across the snow spells, in
   glowing tracks, a giant unmissable arrow pointing at her window.
9. **5s** — The Raccoon plants a flag at the arrow's tip. Hold for end
   card.
   🦝 VO: "You ARE where you walked."

### OUTRO — "The Bounce" (~40s)

1. **10s** — Same snowfield, new walk. Layla pauses BEFORE each step —
   future-self check: a small friendly mirror-Layla (older) appears over
   her shoulder, nods or winces. The grumpy-jab step gets a wince; she
   doesn't take it.
2. **10s** — She stomps deliberate GOOD tracks instead: a helped-friend
   track (two hands), a cool-project track (rocket), a kind-comment
   track (heart). The trail glows warm gold.
3. **10s** — Trail check: she turns and reviews her own tracks like a
   ranger, sweeps ONE old banana-peel track she can still reach into a
   dustpan (some you CAN clean up — check your trail).
4. **5s** — The Raccoon sleds up, magnifying glass ready — and finds a
   golden trail leading to a "GALLERY OF GOOD TRACKS" with HER name on
   it. Tourists (comment-frogs) applaud her exhibits.
   🦝 VO: "This gallery is USELESS to me. It's… lovely."
5. **5s** — Flop-out gag: his own sled-tracks have circled his fort a
   hundred times — the aerial view spells "I LIVE HERE" with an arrow.
   The frogs sell tickets to HIS trail. Gerald takes fifty per cent.

## Week 13 — Screen Time: "The Battery Thief"

**Weakness exploited:** no balance — ignoring the grumpy-tired signs, screens at
bedtime, no plan, no off-switch skills.
**Strength shown:** noticing the signs, screens out of bedtime, making a plan WITH
a grown-up, powering off like a pro.

### INTRO — "The Break-In" (~80s)

1. **10s** — Bedroom, Saturday morning sun. Adam has a personal battery
   meter floating over his head — FULL and green. He's mid-jumping-jack,
   full of beans, tablet on the desk still dark.
2. **10s** — Raccoon's lair-corner: he assembles a comically long bendy
   straw, threading it out his fort window, across the roofs, toward
   Adam's window.
   🦝 VO: "I don't steal batteries. I just… sip."
3. **10s** — Adam picks up the tablet "for a minute". The straw taps
   onto his floating battery meter with a rubber-sucker plunk. As he
   scrolls, the green bar sips downward, tick by tick.
4. **10s** — Signs montage, played big: his eyes go pink-rimmed; his leg
   jiggles; he snaps grumpily at a knock on the door; a snack goes
   uneaten. The battery hits half — amber.
5. **5s** — Outside the window: a football arcs by, kids' laughter.
   Adam's ears don't even twitch. The Raccoon slurps louder.
6. **10s** — Night. The tablet glows under the duvet like a campfire.
   On the meter: the moon icon knocks politely — sleep waiting to start —
   but the screen-light shoos it away.
   🦝 VO: "Sleep hates blue light. And I LOVE that."
7. **10s** — Morning after: Adam rises like a zombie, battery meter
   deep red, hair like a hedge. He reaches for… the tablet again. The
   straw does a happy wiggle.
8. **10s** — Raccoon's corner: shelves of jars, each glowing with sipped
   battery-juice, labelled by day. He tops up "SATURDAY", burps softly.
9. **5s** — He reclines in a deck chair powered by the juice, sunglasses
   on. Hold for end card.
   🦝 VO: "Endless screens. My renewable energy."

### OUTRO — "The Bounce" (~40s)

1. **10s** — Same Saturday sun. Adam scrolls — then catches his OWN leg
   jiggling and his grumpy face in the dark screen reflection. He
   recognises the signs — points at them like a detective: "aha."
2. **10s** — Kitchen table: Adam and a grown-up silhouette draw a plan
   TOGETHER on paper — boxes for game time, football, reading, and a
   big moon zone with the tablet parked OUTSIDE the bedroom in a little
   charging garage. Both sign it like a treaty.
3. **10s** — Power-off like a pro: Adam finishes a level, says goodbye
   to his game pet with a salute, and holds the power button — the
   screen winks out FIRST TRY. He drops the tablet in the garage and
   struts off; the straw sucks air.
   🦝 VO: "AIR! I'm sipping AIR!"
4. **5s** — Night: the bedroom is screen-free and dim; the moon icon
   settles onto Adam's battery meter like a nightcap. The meter refills
   as he sleeps — green by sunrise.
5. **5s** — Flop-out gag: the Raccoon's jars are empty; he tries to sip
   Gerald with the straw. Gerald remains a potato. Fully charged Adam
   sprints past the window with the football.

## Week 14 — Smart Devices: "The House That Listens"

**Weakness exploited:** forgetting smart speakers/TVs/watches/doorbells/toys can
hear and see — saying secrets out loud, never checking settings.
**Strength shown:** knowing what's smart, no secrets out loud, cameras noticed,
settings checked with a grown-up.

### INTRO — "The Break-In" (~80s)

1. **10s** — Bedroom + landing. A cheerful tour: smart speaker on the
   shelf, smart TV in the corner, Layla's smart watch, a robot toy dog,
   the doorbell camera by the front door — each gets a friendly "hello
   there" glow as Layla passes.
2. **10s** — Raccoon's lair-corner mission control: a wall of speakers
   shaped like EARS, each wired to a house on a map. He puts on a
   headset, cracks his knuckles.
   🦝 VO: "Why break in? The house takes NOTES for me."
3. **10s** — Layla and Adam whisper a "secret" in the bedroom — the new
   den's hiding spot, acted out with a treasure-map prop — RIGHT next to
   the smart speaker. Its little light pulses politely with every word.
4. **5s** — In the lair: a paper transcript scrolls out of an ear-
   speaker. The Raccoon reads it wearing opera glasses. Delighted gasp.
5. **10s** — The robot toy dog trundles after the kids from room to
   room. In X-ray view its wagging tail is an antenna, streaming a
   dotted line out the window to the fort.
6. **10s** — Layla tells her smart WATCH her "password rhyme" out loud
   (musical mumble — no real words). The watch face ripples; the lair's
   jars from W13 get a new neighbour: a jar of captured whispers.
   🦝 VO: "Secrets said out loud aren't secrets. They're BROADCASTS."
7. **10s** — Evening: the smart TV camera dot glows over the room like a
   tiny moon while the family watches cartoons; on the lair's monitors,
   the same cartoon — plus the living room — plays picture-in-picture.
8. **10s** — The Raccoon walks his map wall: every device in the house
   now has a red string to his headset. He conducts the strings like an
   orchestra.
9. **5s** — He tips his headset like a cap. Hold for end card.
   🦝 VO: "Thanks for the house tour. I never even knocked."

### OUTRO — "The Bounce" (~40s)

1. **10s** — Same bedroom. Before the den-secret, Layla points a finger
   slowly around the room — spot-the-smart sweep: speaker, TV, watch,
   toy dog. Each device gets a knowing nod. The kids zip lips, then
   whisper the secret OUTSIDE under the real sky, map hugged tight.
2. **10s** — Settings safari with a grown-up silhouette: the speaker's
   mic toggled to a sleeping-ear icon; the toy dog's antenna-tail gets a
   tiny woolly sock; the TV camera gets a sliding cover, closed with a
   satisfying click.
3. **10s** — The doorbell camera stays ON — pointed OUT at the street
   where it belongs. It catches the Raccoon tip-toeing up the path with
   a listening trumpet; the doorbell bongs a cheerful alarm.
   🦝 VO: "The one camera that works… works on ME?!"
4. **5s** — Lair: transcripts print blank page after blank page. The
   whisper jar sits empty. His ear-wall droops like wilted flowers.
5. **5s** — Flop-out gag: the only transcript of the day — Gerald's
   "…" (a potato says nothing). He files it under EVIDENCE anyway.
