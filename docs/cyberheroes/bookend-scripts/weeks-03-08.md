# Bookend Scripts — Weeks 3–8

Format: shot number · seconds · action. `🦝 VO:` lines are the only spoken
audio and are recorded in Callum's voice. See 00-production-canon.md.

## Week 3 — Stranger Danger: "The Friend Who Wasn't"

**Weakness exploited:** believing an online "friend" is who they say.
**Strength shown:** spotting the fake, refusing red-flag requests, telling a grown-up.
*(Safeguarding note: the deception is shown ON THE RACCOON'S side — costume,
props, absurdity. The child character never appears endangered; the win is
his TRICK working, never contact.)*

### INTRO — "The Break-In" (~80s)

1. **10s** — Bedroom, night. Adam plays a space game on the desk monitor. A
   friend request pops on screen: a smiling kid avatar, "STARPILOT_9". Wide
   lock so the avatar stays legible.
2. **10s** — Cut to the Raccoon's lair-corner (cardboard fort, fairy
   lights): the Raccoon in a HUGE fake kid-mask on a stick, typing with two
   paws, surrounded by crumpled "nice kid phrases" cue cards.
   🦝 VO: "Hello fellow child! I also enjoy… homework?"
3. **10s** — Split-screen effect: on the left Adam chats happily; on the
   right the Raccoon reads from cue cards, nodding along, mask slipping.
4. **10s** — The chat window fills with the Raccoon's asks (icons, not
   text): a gift box, a camera, a padlock with a shushing finger. Each icon
   lands with a soft "ping".
5. **5s** — Adam glances at the door (should I tell someone?), then shrugs
   and turns back. The Raccoon's eyes glitter in the monitor reflection.
6. **10s** — Adam drags a photo of his football-team poster toward the
   chat. The cursor hovers… and releases. The file whooshes away.
7. **10s** — The Raccoon's fort: he catches the photo printout in a
   butterfly net, cartwheels, knocks his own mask off mid-celebration.
   🦝 VO: "He never even asked who I REALLY was!"
8. **10s** — The Raccoon pins the photo onto a corkboard of "MY NEW
   FRIENDS" — every single pin is another kid he's fooled, all connected
   with red string to a picture of HIM.
9. **5s** — Slow push on the Raccoon's grin lit by the monitor. Hold for
   end card.
   🦝 VO: "Being a pretend friend is SO much easier than a real one."

### OUTRO — "The Bounce" (~40s)

1. **10s** — Same bedroom, day. Layla is on the monitor now. The same
   "STARPILOT_9" request pops up. She leans in, eyes narrowing — spot-the-
   fake face.
2. **10s** — Layla clicks the profile: brand-new account, zero games
   played, a too-perfect avatar. She holds up three fingers counting red
   flags as icons flash: gift box, camera, shush-padlock.
3. **10s** — She slides the laptop to a grown-up silhouette at the door and
   points. One decisive tap: BLOCKED. The chat window folds itself into a
   paper plane and flies off screen.
4. **5s** — The Raccoon's fort: the paper plane loops back and bops him on
   the nose. His cue cards scatter.
   🦝 VO: "Who told the grown-up?! That's CHEATING!"
5. **5s** — Flop-out gag: his "MY NEW FRIENDS" corkboard slumps off the
   wall; behind it, his only real friend — a potato with googly eyes.
   🦝 VO: "…hello, Gerald."

## Week 4 — Scams & Tricks: "The Golden Ticket"

**Weakness exploited:** falling for too-good-to-be-true + urgency + a fake sender.
**Strength shown:** smelling the trick, checking the sender, showing a grown-up.

### INTRO — "The Break-In" (~80s)

1. **10s** — Bedroom, after school. Layla's tablet chimes: a glittering
   popup — a treasure chest bursting with game coins, "WINNER!" confetti.
   Camera wide, art carries it (no fine text).
2. **10s** — Raccoon's lair-corner: he cranks an old-timey mail cannon
   labelled with a prize-ribbon sticker, firing identical glittering
   envelopes into a tube marked TO: EVERYONE.
   🦝 VO: "Nobody checks who a prize is FROM!"
3. **10s** — Back on the tablet: a countdown ring appears around the chest,
   shrinking. Layla's eyes go wide; she bounces on her toes with hurry-up
   panic.
4. **5s** — Close on the sender line as a PICTURE: a crown badge that, on a
   second look, is crayon-drawn and peeling at the corner. Layla doesn't
   take the second look.
5. **10s** — Layla taps the chest. The screen fills with a cartoon vacuum
   that slurps the coin jar icon from the corner of her game.
6. **10s** — Raccoon's corner: coins rain into his bathtub; he backstrokes
   through them wearing a snorkel.
   🦝 VO: "Hurry-hurry makes them clumsy — works every time!"
7. **10s** — Layla's game shows an empty coin jar with a sad face. She
   turns the tablet upside down, baffled.
8. **10s** — The Raccoon stamps a big red "GULLIBLE" star onto a clipboard
   checklist of tricks: sparkles ✓, countdown ✓, fake crown ✓.
9. **5s** — He polishes a single chocolate coin, bites it, winces — hold
   his smug/sore face for end card.
   🦝 VO: "Almost all treasure. ALMOST."

### OUTRO — "The Bounce" (~40s)

1. **10s** — Same tablet, same glittering chest popup, same countdown.
   Layla freezes mid-reach… then smiles. She's seen this before.
2. **10s** — She flips an imaginary detective cap on, taps the crayon-crown
   sender badge, and it peels off entirely, revealing a purple paw print
   underneath.
3. **10s** — She walks the tablet to the kitchen doorway and shows a
   grown-up silhouette. Together they press one button; the popup deflates
   like a balloon and whizzes around the room.
4. **5s** — Raccoon's corner: his mail cannon coughs, then fires every
   envelope BACK into his fort, burying him to the ears.
   🦝 VO: "Return to sender?! Since WHEN?!"
5. **5s** — Flop-out gag: he emerges wearing the "GULLIBLE" star stuck to
   his own forehead. Gerald the potato watches, judging.

## Week 5 — Cyberbullying: "The Meanness Machine"

**Weakness exploited:** mean messages landing, fighting back, and passing it on.
**Strength shown:** it's not your fault, don't retaliate, don't forward — tell someone.
*(Tone note: the meanness targets ADAM'S GAME CASTLE, not his body/face/
identity — concrete, repairable, age-safe. Sad beats stay short; the film
turns to warmth fast.)*

### INTRO — "The Break-In" (~80s)

1. **10s** — Bedroom, evening. Adam proudly screenshots a blocky castle he
   built in a building game. String lights glow; he beams.
2. **10s** — Raccoon's lair-corner: a rusty machine labelled with a storm-
   cloud icon — he feeds ONE mean message card in, and the machine prints
   dozens, like angry confetti.
   🦝 VO: "One mean word, MANY mean echoes. Efficiency!"
3. **10s** — Adam's screen: laughing-face reactions pile onto his castle
   post like hailstones. Each "plink" dims the string lights a notch.
4. **5s** — Adam's face falls. He looks at his OWN hands like he did
   something wrong. (Beat lands "it feels like your fault" — briefly.)
5. **10s** — Adam angrily types back — a storm-cloud of his own. The
   machine in the Raccoon's corner GROWS a size, delighted, sprouting a
   second funnel.
6. **10s** — A classmate avatar forwards the mean post onward; tiny
   storm-clouds hop from screen to screen across a map of houses.
   🦝 VO: "And the best part — the kids deliver it FOR me!"
7. **10s** — The bedroom is dim now, string lights nearly out. Adam sits
   with the tablet face-down, alone. Rain taps the window.
8. **10s** — Raccoon's corner: he lounges on a throne of printed mean-
   cards, sipping juice from a tiny umbrella cup.
9. **5s** — Push on the machine's dial: it points at a growing storm
   cloud. Hold for end card.
   🦝 VO: "Storms only grow if everyone keeps feeding them."

### OUTRO — "The Bounce" (~40s)

1. **10s** — Same post, same hailstone reactions begin — but Adam sets the
   tablet DOWN, walks to the door, and brings back Layla and a grown-up
   silhouette. The room brightens a notch just from company.
2. **10s** — Adam does NOT type back. He shows the screen; the grown-up
   nods; one calm tap. The storm-cloud cards flutter into a bin that seals
   shut. String lights re-brighten notch by notch.
3. **10s** — Layla gets the same forwarded storm-cloud — and lets it stop
   with her: she pops it like a bubble. Across the house-map, other kids
   pop theirs too. The chain dies out house by house.
4. **5s** — Raccoon's corner: his Meanness Machine sputters, shrinks to
   hamster-size, and squeaks out one soggy card.
   🦝 VO: "It only works if they PASS IT ON!"
5. **5s** — Flop-out gag: the machine dispenses its final product — a
   card reading a big thumbs-up icon. The Raccoon faints. Gerald approves.

## Week 6 — Gaming Safety: "The In-Game Impostor"

**Weakness exploited:** open chat with anyone, real info in game chat, "let's chat
somewhere else", ignoring report/block, and a "free download" trap.
**Strength shown:** friends-only settings, keeping real life out of chat, staying
in-game, using report & block.

### INTRO — "The Break-In" (~80s)

1. **10s** — Bedroom, weekend morning. Adam and Layla co-op on the desk
   monitor — a bright mining game. Their in-game names float over blocky
   avatars.
2. **10s** — Raccoon's lair-corner: he steps INTO a cardboard "player
   costume" — a blocky knight suit two sizes too big — and clanks into the
   game world.
   🦝 VO: "Open lobby! They'll talk to ANY helmet."
3. **10s** — In-game: the knight sidles up, emote-waving. Chat bubbles with
   icons only: a house, a school bell, a birthday cake — and Adam's avatar
   answers each with a cheerful thumbs-up.
4. **10s** — The knight drops a glowing gift chest labelled with a
   "FREE!" starburst. Inside, a wriggling purple bug grins.
5. **5s** — Layla's avatar points at the knight's REPORT flag hovering
   overhead… Adam waves her off — he's nice!
6. **10s** — The knight beckons them toward a swirling purple door marked
   with a different app's chat-balloon logo shape: "somewhere quieter."
   🦝 VO: "Step out of the castle, little knights…"
7. **10s** — Adam's avatar takes one step through. The game's friendly
   music warps; the bright world greys at the edges of the door.
8. **10s** — Raccoon's corner: monitors bloom with Adam's collected
   details pinned to a map — house, school bell, cake — the bug from the
   chest chews happily on the game's coin sack.
9. **5s** — The knight helmet tips back; purple ears pop out. Hold the
   grin for end card.
   🦝 VO: "GG. That means Got'em Good."

### OUTRO — "The Bounce" (~40s)

1. **10s** — Same game, new day. Settings screen FIRST: Layla toggles a
   big friendly switch to a two-heads "friends only" icon. The lobby gate
   politely closes on the queueing knight.
2. **10s** — The knight squeezes in anyway on an old invite. Icon-asks
   fill the chat — house, bell, cake — Adam answers each with a zipped-lip
   emote. The knight's shoulders slump.
3. **10s** — The purple "somewhere quieter" door appears; both kids' avatars
   plant a NO sign in front of it, then Layla slams the big REPORT flag
   and Adam the BLOCK shield, in sync.
4. **5s** — The knight costume is ejected from the game like a arcade
   prize-claw miss, cartwheeling into the Raccoon's fort.
   🦝 VO: "Reported?! I was being SO knightly!"
5. **5s** — Flop-out gag: his "free" gift chest opens in his own lap —
   the purple bug eats HIS coin sack, burps, and waves. Gerald waves back.

## Week 7 — In-Game Spending: "The Coin Vacuum"

**Weakness exploited:** not realising coins are real money, loot-box gambling,
FOMO pressure, buying without asking, "free currency" scams.
**Strength shown:** coins = money, spotting the gamble, asking first, ignoring "free".

### INTRO — "The Break-In" (~80s)

1. **10s** — Bedroom. Layla plays a pet-collecting game on the tablet. A
   dazzling shop tab pulses: rainbow eggs, a giant "MEGA DEAL" ribbon,
   fireworks.
2. **10s** — Raccoon's lair-corner: he wears a carnival barker's hat,
   spinning a wheel of identical rainbow eggs — every slice of the wheel
   is labelled with the SAME sad rubber duck.
   🦝 VO: "Spin enough times, they forget it's money!"
3. **10s** — Layla taps an egg. Coins fly out of her jar into the screen —
   and the egg hatches… a plain rubber duck. She blinks, taps again.
   Another duck.
4. **5s** — A timer ribbon slams across the shop: "MEGA DEAL ENDS SOON!"
   with a shaking alarm clock. Layla's tapping speeds up.
5. **10s** — Cut wide: with each tap, real coins levitate out of a piggy
   bank on the SHELF behind her, forming a conga line into the tablet.
   She never looks back at it.
6. **10s** — A popup offers a treasure van: "FREE 10,000 COINS — just
   enter your grown-up's card!" The van's licence plate is a paw print.
   🦝 VO: "Free coins! The most expensive kind!"
7. **10s** — Raccoon's corner: the coin conga line rains into a claw-foot
   bath. He ducks (pun intended) as rubber ducks pile beside him — his
   own loot, worthless even to him.
8. **10s** — Layla's pet game: a stable full of identical sad ducks. Her
   coin jar shows moths. The piggy bank on the shelf is see-through empty.
9. **5s** — The Raccoon bites a coin — this one's real. Slow grin for the
   end card.
   🦝 VO: "Game coins, real money. MY favourite exchange rate."

### OUTRO — "The Bounce" (~40s)

1. **10s** — Same shop tab, same MEGA DEAL fireworks. Layla reaches — then
   stops and looks BACK at the piggy bank on the shelf. She physically
   picks it up and sets it next to the tablet: now she can see both.
2. **10s** — The rainbow egg wheel appears; she squints and sees the trick
   — every slice, same duck. She swipes it away like closing a curtain.
3. **10s** — The "FREE 10,000 COINS" van screeches in. Layla walks the
   tablet to the door and holds it up to a grown-up silhouette. Together
   they tap NO; the van's wheels fall off.
   🦝 VO: "Not the ASKING! Anything but the asking!"
4. **5s** — She buys ONE small thing she actually wanted — with a nod from
   the doorway — and her pet does a happy dance. Piggy bank barely dips.
5. **5s** — Flop-out gag: the Raccoon's bath drains to reveal it was
   mostly ducks all along; three coins remain. He pays one to Gerald.
   🦝 VO: "Gerald, we're ruined."

## Week 8 — Photos & Videos: "The Picture Detective"

**Weakness exploited:** a shared photo is out forever, and one photo leaks school,
street and location without a word typed.
**Strength shown:** think before sharing, ask before posting others, strip the clues.

### INTRO — "The Break-In" (~80s)

1. **10s** — Bedroom, golden afternoon. Adam in his football kit takes a
   grinning selfie by the window; behind him: his school crest on the kit,
   a street sign through the glass, house number on the door frame.
2. **10s** — He posts it. The photo lifts off the phone as a paper
   photograph and DUPLICATES mid-air — two, four, eight — copies flutter
   out the window like pigeons.
3. **10s** — Raccoon's lair-corner, detective mode: deerstalker hat,
   giant magnifying glass, corkboard. One photo-pigeon lands in his paw.
   🦝 VO: "One photo. A THOUSAND clues. Elementary."
4. **10s** — Magnifying glass sweeps the photo: school crest circled →
   red string to a school flyer. Street sign circled → string to a map.
   The corkboard builds itself.
5. **5s** — He flips the photo over: a glowing location pin peels off the
   back like a sticker. He licks it and sticks it on the map. Bullseye.
6. **10s** — Adam, at home, thinks better of it and hits delete. The
   phone's copy vanishes — but through the window we see the pigeon-copies
   still perched on every rooftop. Delete didn't reach them.
7. **10s** — One pigeon-copy lands on a classmate's screen — it's a photo
   WITH Layla in the background mid-sneeze. She never said yes to that.
   Her sneeze face gets a laughing sticker slapped on it.
8. **10s** — Raccoon's corner: the finished corkboard — school, street,
   house number, pin — connected in red string to a printed map with a
   flag planted on it. He dusts his paws.
   🦝 VO: "He typed NOTHING. The photo did all the talking."
9. **5s** — He frames Adam's selfie in a "EMPLOYEE OF THE MONTH" frame on
   his wall. Hold for end card.

### OUTRO — "The Bounce" (~40s)

1. **10s** — Same golden window, new photo moment. Adam raises the phone —
   then LOOKS at the frame first: crest, street sign, door number. He
   steps two paces left; now it's just sky and posters behind him.
2. **10s** — Layla wanders into frame; Adam lowers the phone and ASKS
   with a gesture. She strikes a deliberate superhero pose — consent both
   ways — thumbs up, snap.
3. **10s** — Before posting: he flips the photo over and peels the glowing
   location pin off HIMSELF, dropping it in the bin. Then posts. ONE
   photo-pigeon flies — and it's clueless: sky, posters, two heroes.
4. **5s** — Raccoon's corner: magnifying glass sweeps the new photo…
   nothing. String goes nowhere. The corkboard collapses under one limp
   thread.
   🦝 VO: "Sky?! POSTERS?! Where are my CLUES?!"
5. **5s** — Flop-out gag: his only find — Adam's thumbs-up, enlarged 500%.
   He pins it up anyway. Gerald gets a matching frame.
