# /cyberexplorers Landing — Acceptance Criteria v1

Benchmark: the /cyberheroes landing's energy, translated to Signal Room. Every criterion is checked against full-page screenshots at 1440px before a version ships. Fail any → redevelop, don't rationalize.

## A. Cinematic energy
1. **Living backdrop** — ambient motion visible in every viewport-height of the page (radar/streams/nodes), never a dead frame.
2. **A set-piece every ~1.5 viewports** — a non-text visual anchor (demo monitor, ID card, incident strip, dossier wall, stamp moment). Three consecutive text-panel sections = fail.
3. **Hero wow in 3 seconds** — motion (descramble + demo playing) and a focal object visible above the fold; the demo monitor must read as *the product playing itself*, cinematic bezel, not a small quiet card.

## B. Hierarchy & scale
4. **Three distinct type scales** per viewport: display (≥56px at desktop), section (28–40px), body (14–17px). Uniform mid-size mono everywhere = fail.
5. **Accent-word treatment** on display lines (gradient or color shift) — the Heroes ACCENT_TEXT move, in cyan→brass register.
6. **Section rhythm** — alternate contained panels with full-bleed moments; adjacent sections must not share identical background treatment.

## C. Color & material
7. **Each section owns an ambient wash** (cyan / band-colors / brass / green) — large soft radial glows, not only 1px accents. Page-wide sameness of navy panels = fail.
8. **Signature materials at scale** — manila/paper and brass appear as large objects (dossiers, ID card, stamps), not just chips.

## D. Register (locked)
9. No emoji, no springs/bounce, no Matrix-rain cliché; snap/slide easing only; US English; reading feel = competence, not menace.
10. Trust line + ICO promises above the fold; SIMULATE hard line stated in parent language.

## E. Craft floor (ui-ux-pro-max)
11. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast on its surface; interactive elements have cursor-pointer + visible hover feedback with no layout shift; touch targets ≥44px; focus states visible.
12. `prefers-reduced-motion` yields a fully readable, static page (content identical).
13. No horizontal scroll at 375/768/1024/1440; text columns ≤ ~75ch.

## F. Conversion skeleton
14. CTA visible in hero, mid-page, and footer; waitlist form functional; stats/proof modules present; parent-reassurance section present; Heroes cross-link present.
