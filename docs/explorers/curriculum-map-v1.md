## 3. Verb policy

Debuts one at a time, each in a mission whose concept *needs* it: TRACE M02 · SIMULATE M06 (post-ARC-Code, per the locked gate) · BUILD M11 · CIPHER M12. INSPECT/DECIDE/PROFILE shipped in M01. Within a mission the three cycle verbs + the incident's dominant verb are all distinct (template rule). The per-mission verb triples below are **DEFAULT** — build-time freedom to swap for variety, the concepts are the locked part. SIMULATE always *predicts and counters*, never authors (LOCKED hard line).

## 4. BLOCK 1 — SIGNALS (M01–05 → CONFIDENTIAL)
*The analyst's foundation: any artifact can be read. Evidence over vibes.*

**M01 · The 24-Hour Threat · PHANTOM HOOK ① — SHIPPED, conforms.**
Cycles: urgency as a tool aimed at reflexes · domain anatomy (read right-to-left) · the M.O. concept. Verbs I/P/D + Second Wave incident (triage → containment → transmit). ARC Code signs at end of briefing, first run (template DECIDE 4 → **now DEFAULT here**). Transfer: don't touch rushed links; check in the official app. Breadcrumb ①: K-STATIC/COORD routing tag (shipped).
*Dedupe: Heroes W4 taught "hurry = trick, show a grown-up"; this teaches WHY pressure works and HOW addresses lie.*

**M02 · Too Good To Be True · SIREN ① — TRACE debut.**
Cycles: (1) incentive analysis — "free" is a price; what the prize funnel buys with your excitement *(deepens Heroes W7 "free currency is a scam" → the funnel mechanics)* (2) TRACE: one campaign, many costumes — follow the giveaway from game chat → DM → claim-site (3) form harvesting — what "claim your prize" actually collects, and why each field is money. Verbs: TRACE/INSPECT/DECIDE. Incident: **The Prize Factory** — assemble the campaign on the evidence board, then cut its hub. Transfer: never enter details on a page a message sent you to; real giveaways live on official channels. Dossier: SIREN ①.

**M03 · The Guessing Game · SKELETON KEY ①.**
Cycles: (1) how guessing actually works — dictionaries, patterns, why `Millie2013!` falls in seconds; *the child watches SK's rig run and analyzes it — never runs one* (Redoubt boundary) *(deepens Heroes W1 rules → mechanism)* (2) reuse is a master key — one breach opens every door (breach concept, kid-scale) (3) passphrases that survive — length beats cleverness; three-random-words evolved. Verbs: INSPECT (audit a password set)/PROFILE/DECIDE (triage which account to fix first). Incident: **Ten Thousand Doors** — the rig hits a wall of doors; reinforce the weakest in priority order. Transfer: change your one reused password this week (with a parent where accounts are shared).

**M04 · The Puzzle You Posted · PACKRAT ①.**
Cycles: (1) the assembly attack — how strangers combine crumbs (handle + photo + bio = school, name, schedule); run as an **authorized ARC audit of a volunteer test account** — defensive framing, never "how to stalk" (2) what a photo leaks beyond its subject — uniforms, reflections, street signs, location data *(deepens Heroes W2/W8 "don't share" → how crumbs combine)* (3) handle hygiene — the same username everywhere makes you linkable. Verbs: TRACE (assemble the audit)/INSPECT (the leaky photo)/DECIDE (what comes down first). Incident: **Paper Trail** — PACKRAT is auctioning a compiled dossier; invalidate it by scrubbing its sources in time. Transfer: audit your own top three posts with the mission checklist.

**M05 · Signal Storm · PHANTOM HOOK ② — BLOCK FINALE.**
Cycles: (1) the costume change — the same M.O. across SMS, QR, DMs *(mechanism home for Heroes W16's "look before you click")* (2) spoofed senders — display name vs address; "from" is a costume too *(deepens Heroes W4 fake senders → header level)* (3) the analyst's queue — triage by severity when volume is the weapon. Verbs: INSPECT/PROFILE (match every variant to ONE actor)/DECIDE. Incident: **The Flood** — a multi-channel wall; then find the one message that isn't spray but *spear* (it knows your name). **CONFIDENTIAL ceremony.** Breadcrumb ②: the spear contains data only PACKRAT could have sold — the actors are connected.

## 5. BLOCK 2 — THE HUMAN FACTOR (M06–10 → SECRET)
*The attacker's real target is a brain. Learn the levers; think one move ahead.*

**M06 · Levers · SIREN ② — SIMULATE debut.**
Cycles: (1) the six pressure levers — hurry, scarcity, authority, liking, fear, payback (kid-worded lever board; the tier's core mental model) (2) SIMULATE: predict which lever comes next in a live con — anticipation as defense (3) naming the lever kills it — the counter-move is calling it out loud. Verbs: SIMULATE/INSPECT (mark levers in transcripts)/DECIDE. Incident: **The Carnival** — rapid mini-cons; call the lever before each reveal. Transfer: name one lever out loud this week (ads count).

**M07 · Borrowed Faces · MIMIC ①.**
Cycles: (1) the hijacked friend — the message really IS from their account; accounts get stolen, trust doesn't transfer *(deepens Heroes W3 fake profiles → compromised real ones)* (2) out-of-band verification — check on a different channel; the home of the tier's most-used protocol (3) the weird-ask detector — real friends don't ask for codes, money, or secrecy. Verbs: INSPECT/DECIDE/PROFILE. Incident: **Two Best Friends** — two identical chats, one hijacked; the protocol tells them apart. Transfer: agree a check-question with your best friend.

**M08 · The Perfect Message · GHOSTWRITER ①.**
Cycles: (1) the old tells are dead — grammar and polish prove nothing now (2) what machines still get wrong — specificity without relationship, warmth without history, everything-at-once flattery (taught as *clues*, not a reliable detector) (3) therefore: verify by source, never by style (ties to M07's protocol). Verbs: INSPECT/SIMULATE (predict the follow-up message)/DECIDE. Incident: **The Pen Pal** — a warm persona that never existed, farming trust toward a small "favor" (money/codes framing — scam, not relationship; the protective grooming frame stays Heroes W3/W11). Transfer: one verify-by-source this week.

**M09 · The Long Game · SIREN ③.**
Cycles: (1) scams are a business — conversion thinking, why scale means *you're* not special, and why that's liberating (2) trust-farming — small gifts, weeks of niceness, the sunk-cost trap ("you've come this far") (3) exit skills — leaving a con mid-way without shame; the con is the con's fault; plus the blackmail lever handled abstractly: *if anyone says they'll share something embarrassing unless you comply — never pay, never comply, tell an adult immediately, you are not in trouble* (see §11, DECIDE 1). Verbs: TRACE (timeline of a weeks-long con)/PROFILE/DECIDE. Incident: **The Collector** — find the moment the friendly account turns the ask. Transfer: the pause-and-tell rule for any ask touching money, codes, or secrets.

**M10 · The Voice · MIMIC ② — BLOCK FINALE.**
Cycles: (1) voice cloning — seconds of audio is enough; the landing-page promise, delivered (2) deepfake video — today's tells AND why tells decay; the durable defense is verification, not spotting (3) **the family code word** — out-of-band verification for emergencies, rehearsed. Verbs: INSPECT (real/fake media set)/SIMULATE (what will the clone ask for?)/DECIDE. Incident: **Caller ID** — an urgent call in a voice you love; run the protocol calmly. **SECRET ceremony.** Breadcrumb ③: the clone was trained on scraped audio (PACKRAT) reading a script (GHOSTWRITER) — three actors, one supply chain. Transfer: set the family code word this week (the course's single most valuable take-home).

## 6. BLOCK 3 — SYSTEMS (M11–15 → TOP SECRET)
*How the machinery actually works — and how to build defenses that hold.*

**M11 · The Master Key · SKELETON KEY ② — BUILD debut.**
Cycles: (1) why your brain can't do this job — unique × strong × forty accounts is a tool's job (2) BUILD: the password manager — generate, store, one strong master passphrase (3) 2FA — the second lock; codes are keys and NOBODY gets asked for keys (ties M07's weird-ask). Verbs: BUILD/INSPECT (which accounts lack the second lock)/DECIDE. Incident: **Locksmith's Nightmare** — SK returns with the M03 rig and *bounces*; the landing's "watch them hold," on screen. Transfer: turn on 2FA on your most important account, with a parent.

**M12 · Unreadable · PACKRAT ② — CIPHER debut.**
Cycles: (1) codes, ciphers, keys — make and break a Caesar; why simple ciphers fall (2) encryption in your day — the padlock; sealed envelopes vs postcards *(new ground — Heroes never touched crypto)* (3) who can read your messages — in-transit vs end-to-end (lite), and why sketchy public Wi-Fi is a postcard party. Verbs: CIPHER/INSPECT/DECIDE. Incident: **The Intercept** — PACKRAT sniffs the coffee-shop Wi-Fi; get the messages home sealed. Transfer: look for the padlock; prefer known networks.

**M13 · Backdoors · SKELETON KEY ③.**
Cycles: (1) recovery is the back door — security questions are guessable from public crumbs (direct callback to M04 — cross-actor synthesis) (2) strategic answers — security answers don't have to be TRUE, they have to be UNGUESSABLE; recovery email/phone freshness (3) session hygiene — logged-in devices, logout on shared machines *(deepens Heroes W18 rule → session concept)*. Verbs: BUILD (harden recovery)/TRACE (how SK chained crumbs to answers)/DECIDE. Incident: **The Side Door** — SK ignores your beautiful password entirely; slam the recovery path. Transfer: fix the security questions on one account.

**M14 · The Update Trap · GHOSTWRITER ②.**
Cycles: (1) a download is a set of powers — permissions as capabilities; the flashlight app that wants your contacts *(deepens Heroes W9 "why does it need that?" → capability model)* (2) updates are armor — what patching actually fixes and why attackers love the un-updated (3) the fake-installer lure — GW packages malware in Block-1 lure clothing. Verbs: INSPECT (permission manifests)/DECIDE (install, deny, investigate)/SIMULATE (predict what the fake installer does next). Incident: **Trojan Delivery** — triage a batch of pending installs. Transfer: run your updates; audit one app's permissions.

**M15 · The Real Site · MIMIC ③ — BLOCK FINALE.**
Cycles: (1) pixel-perfect fakes — a login page's LOOK cannot authenticate it (2) what can: the address bar — browser chrome vs page content (the ARC-chrome art rule, taught out loud as browser literacy) (3) the manager as lie detector — it won't autofill on the wrong domain; your M11 tool detects what your eyes can't (synthesis: M01 domains + M11 tools). Verbs: INSPECT/BUILD/DECIDE. Incident: **Hall of Mirrors** — five login pages, one real; trust the tools. **TOP SECRET ceremony.** Breadcrumb ④: every mirror site registered by the same hidden entity — the dossier board now links all six actors to one signature.

## 7. BLOCK 4 — THE LONG GAME (M16–20 → ULTRA)
*Identity, integrity, ethics — and the arc closes.*

**M16 · The File On You · PACKRAT ③.**
Cycles: (1) the data economy — trackers and brokers, kid-scale: "companies keep a file about you and trade it" *(deepens Heroes W12 tracks-in-snow → the market for tracks)* (2) the self-audit — M04's method turned formally on yourself (3) curation and takedowns — deleting, correcting, locking down; making good tracks on purpose *(Heroes W12's agency move, tier-up)*. Verbs: TRACE (your own trail)/BUILD (privacy hardening pass)/DECIDE. Incident: **The Auction** — PACKRAT's final sale; poison the file by killing its sources before the gavel. Transfer: a privacy-settings audit with a parent — and yes, theirs is worse than yours.

**M17 · Ghost Stories · GHOSTWRITER ③.**
Cycles: (1) synthetic everything — images, screenshots, reviews made to order; the provenance question: *who benefits if I believe this?* (2) lateral reading — verify the claim in a second tab, not the page on its own terms *(deepens Heroes W15 "AI can be wrong" → a verification workflow)* (3) AI in your corner — using it to check while knowing it confidently lies. Verbs: INSPECT (synthetic media set)/TRACE (source chains)/SIMULATE (which claim gets attacked next — predict targets, never author fakes). Incident: **The Rumor Mill** — a fake screenshot rips through the school group chat; trace it, debunk it, contain it kindly (forwarding = amplifying; *Heroes W5's bystander idea, mechanized*). Transfer: lateral-read one viral thing before sharing it.

**M18 · The Recruiter · K-STATIC/COORD, veiled.**
Cycles: (1) dual-use — your skills could run cons now; the ARC Code is the difference between analyst and actor (why we predict but never author) (2) responsible disclosure — you found a flaw or a leak (a classmate's logged-in account, an open group): report it, never exploit it, never shame them (3) how kids actually get recruited into cybercrime — "small favors," account borrowing, money moving; recognizing the recruitment pitch is an elite skill, and the pitch deconstructs into M06 levers. Verbs: DECIDE (the temptation, branched)/PROFILE (the recruiter — a first, veiled contact with the coordinator)/INSPECT. Incident: **The Offer** — walk away clean and file the report; the report becomes the thread that finds the coordinator. Breadcrumb ⑤: the recruiter's channel signature matches ①–④. Transfer: money for favors with accounts or codes = recruitment; tell an adult the same day. The ARC Code, re-signed — this time it means something.

**M19 · Static Rising · ALL ACTORS (PHANTOM HOOK ③ leads).**
Cycles: (1) campaign anatomy — recon → lure → access → harvest, each phase mapped to the actor who owns it (the whole course revised by construction) (2) defense in depth — your layered defenses as one system; every layer catches what the last one missed (3) incident command — triage, contain, report, communicate, for a whole-school scenario. Verbs: TRACE (the campaign map)/SIMULATE (call the next phase before it fires — the payoff)/DECIDE. Incident: **All Frequencies** — the coordinated wave, every actor's interference signature appearing in sequence; hold the line phase by phase. Breadcrumb: the campaign's cadence reveals where the coordinator transmits from.

**M20 · Signal Zero · K-STATIC/COORD — CAPSTONE. No new concepts (Heroes W20 precedent).**
Recombination cycles: (1) the identity puzzle — all five breadcrumbs on one TRACE mega-board (2) the final lure — a spear-phish built from everything "you" ever leaked in the course fiction; the hardest INSPECT in the tier (3) the handoff — you don't punish; you document and hand to the adults and the platform (escalation modeled at mastery, DECIDE). Incident: **Signal Zero** — the coordinator floods every channel; the operative runs the full discipline solo. ARC chrome never corrupts, even now (locked). Ends: dossier wall complete, coordinator unmasked (identity fiction → DECIDE 2), **ULTRA ceremony, the card reprint**. Transfer: you're the family's analyst now — run a family security night *(Heroes W19's role-flip, tier-up)*.
