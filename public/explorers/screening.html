CYBER EXPLORERS — BLOCK 1 SCREENING

The new story shape: a one-time recruitment opener, then each block is a problem that erupts, the lesson, and a win the learner earns.
OPENER → PROBLEM (intro) → LESSON → SUCCESS (outro)
OPENER · ONCE"The Save" — recruitment Plays before Block 1. He spots a scam on instinct, saves a friend, and ARC scouts him.
BLOCK 1 · INTRO"The Wave" — the problem A phishing wave hits the school and hooks Maya. Instinct isn't enough. "Now you learn to read the signals." Ends unresolved.
▼   THE LEARNER PLAYS THE BLOCK 1 LESSON — spotting phishing signals   ▼
BLOCK 1 · OUTRO"Reeled In" — the success Now able to read the signals, the hero traces and shuts down HOOK's wave, frees the accounts, saves Maya. A STATIC mark tags the finish.

BLOCK 2 — THE HUMAN FACTOR

Villain: SIREN, the social engineer. She hacks people, not computers.
BLOCK 2 · INTRO"The Voice" — the problem SIREN's manipulation sweeps in: an urgent message pressures Maya to hand over a code. It's not a fake link the hero can just spot. "She hacks people. Learn how they push you." Ends unresolved.
▼   THE LEARNER PLAYS THE BLOCK 2 LESSON — resisting manipulation   ▼
BLOCK 2 · OUTRO"Called Out" — the success Knowing the levers (urgency, authority, fear), the hero stays cool, warns Maya, and she refuses. SIREN is caught out. "You can't con the prepared."

BLOCK 3 — THE SYSTEMS

Villain: MIMIC, the Faceless. Deepfakes and impersonation: seeing isn't believing.
BLOCK 3 · INTRO"Not Me" — the problem MIMIC wears the hero's own face to fool Maya. He can't prove which one is real. "Stop trusting your eyes. Check who's really there." Ends unresolved.
▼   THE LEARNER PLAYS THE BLOCK 3 LESSON — verifying identity & spotting fakes   ▼
BLOCK 3 · OUTRO"Face Off" — the success The hero sets an identity check, asks a question only the real one could answer, and MIMIC's mask shatters. "Seeing isn't believing. Proof is."
